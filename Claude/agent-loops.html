Stopping conditions

  • • Goal achieved successfully
  • • Maximum iteration or time limit reached
  • • Token or budget limit exceeded
  • • Tool failure or unrecoverable error
  • • Human approval or intervention required

Always define iteration limits and safe failure handling.
