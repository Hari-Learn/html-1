🤖 Claude Models

Opus • Sonnet • Haiku • Model Selection

1. Claude Model Family

Claude has different model families designed for different levels of capability, speed, and workload requirements.

Task → Requirements → Choose Appropriate Model

Opus

High capability for complex reasoning, demanding coding, analysis, and difficult tasks.

Think: Complex

Sonnet

Balances capability, speed, and cost for many production workloads.

Think: Balance

Haiku

Designed for fast, efficient and high-throughput workloads.

Think: Fast & Efficient

2. Model Selection

  • Task complexity
  • Required quality
  • Latency requirements
  • Cost constraints
  • Request volume

3. Exam Example

Classify 1 million simple customer messages as complaint / not complaint.

High volume + simple task + speed → Consider an efficient model such as Haiku.
🧠 Remember: Opus = Complex | Sonnet = Balance | Haiku = Fast & Efficient
