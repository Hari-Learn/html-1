AI Agent Architecture

Agent Life Cycle

The agent life cycle describes how an AI agent receives a goal, plans and executes tasks, evaluates results, and continuously improves.

01

Understand

Receive the user's goal, context, constraints, and available information.

02

Plan

Break the goal into smaller tasks and determine the steps needed.

03

Act

Use tools, APIs, data, or other systems to execute the planned tasks.

04

Observe

Collect tool outputs, system responses, and other results from execution.

05

Evaluate

Check whether the result satisfies the goal and identify errors or gaps.

06

Respond

Deliver the final result, explain relevant outcomes, and finish the task.

Agent Loop

Understand → Plan → Act → Observe → Evaluate → Respond
