Structured Outputs

Turn AI responses into predictable data.

What are Structured Outputs?

Structured outputs format responses using a defined schema, such as JSON, making results easier for applications to validate and process.

  "topic": "Claude AI",
  "difficulty": "beginner",

Prompt Chaining

Break a complex task into smaller prompts where each step uses the previous result.

STEP 01

Research

Collect relevant facts.

STEP 02

Analyze

Identify patterns and insights.

STEP 03

Generate

Produce the final report.

Task Decomposition Example

Task: Build a market research report.

  • Define the research question.
  • Gather and verify information.
  • Analyze competitors and trends.
  • Return findings in a specified JSON schema.
