Human-in-the-Loop

When should an agent ask a human before proceeding?

What Is HITL?

Human-in-the-Loop means a human participates in an automated workflow at a defined point.

The human may review, approve, reject or correct an action.

When Is Approval Needed?

  • High-value financial transactions
  • Deleting important records
  • Policy exceptions
  • High-impact decisions
  • Uncertain or sensitive actions

Approval Workflow

Prepare → Pause → Human Review → Approve or Reject → Continue

The agent should not execute the protected action before approval.

What Should Human See?

  • What action is requested?
  • Why is it needed?
  • What is the expected impact?
  • Relevant supporting information
  • Approve and reject options

If Approval Is Rejected

Do not execute the action.

Explain the outcome and offer a safe alternative if possible.

Record the decision for auditing when appropriate.

Important Safeguard

Approval must be linked to the exact action and its parameters.

If the amount, recipient or action changes, obtain approval again when required.

Exam Insight

For sensitive or irreversible actions, pause execution and obtain appropriate human approval. Enforce approval in the application/backend, not only through a prompt.
