Grounded Answers & Citations

Generate answers supported by retrieved documents instead of unsupported claims.

Example Answer

Question: How long is the refund period?

Answer: Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Source: [1] Refund Policy, page 4

Illustrative example — verify against the actual source.

Grounding Checklist

✓ Retrieve relevant source passages.

✓ Answer only what the evidence supports.

✓ Attach citations to supported claims.

✓ State when evidence is missing or insufficient.

Question → Retrieval → Evidence → Answer + Citations
