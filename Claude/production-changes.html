Production Engineering

Production Changes

Make every production change deliberate, reviewable, and easy to reverse.

Before the Change

  • • Define scope, risk, and success criteria.
  • • Review the change with another engineer.
  • • Confirm backups and rollback steps.

After the Change

  • • Verify health checks and key metrics.
  • • Watch logs for unexpected errors.
  • • Document the outcome and follow-ups.
