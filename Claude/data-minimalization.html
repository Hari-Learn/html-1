Data Quality

Authoritative Data Sources

Give AI systems access to trusted, current, and clearly governed sources instead of allowing unreliable information to drive decisions.

01

Prefer First-Party Sources

Use official databases, internal systems, published policies, and verified APIs whenever they are available.

02

Track Provenance

Record where information came from, when it was retrieved, and which version or authority supplied it.

03

Resolve Conflicts

Establish a clear source hierarchy so conflicting information does not lead to arbitrary or inconsistent decisions.

Trusted

Verified official source

Current

Fresh and maintained data

Traceable

Clear provenance and ownership
