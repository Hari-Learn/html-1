Web Search & Research

Use web search for current information and research workflows to investigate complex questions across multiple sources.

Research workflow

🔍 1. Define — Form a clear research question.
🌐 2. Search — Find relevant, credible sources.
📖 3. Compare — Check evidence and conflicting claims.
✅ 4. Synthesize — Summarize findings with citations.

Important skills

  • Write specific search questions.
  • Prefer authoritative and recent sources.
  • Verify dates, statistics, and quotations.
  • Distinguish facts, opinions, and uncertainty.
  • Check citations against the original source.

Try this prompt

Research recent developments in RAG. Compare at least three credible sources, include publication dates and citations, and identify unanswered questions.
