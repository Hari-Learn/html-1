Citations & RAG

Making Claude answers traceable to supporting information.

1. What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) retrieves relevant information and supplies it to Claude before generating an answer.

ELI10: Instead of answering from memory, Claude first looks up the relevant pages in a library.

2. RAG Workflow

User asks a question

↓

Retrieve relevant documents

↓

Provide retrieved content to Claude

↓

Generate grounded answer

3. What are Citations?

Citations identify the source passages supporting an answer.

Answer: Employees receive 20 days of annual leave.

Source: HR Policy, page 12, paragraph 3

Claude's API supports citation features for supported document inputs and formats. Citation support depends on the input method.

4. Why Citations Matter

  • Users can verify information.
  • Answers become easier to audit.
  • Unsupported claims are easier to identify.
  • Useful for legal, financial and policy documents.

A citation does not automatically guarantee that a claim is correct. Check whether the cited passage actually supports it.

5. Exam Scenario

A company wants Claude to answer questions from internal HR policies and show the exact supporting passages. Which approach is suitable?

Reveal answer

Use RAG to retrieve relevant policy content and provide citations or source references so users can verify the answer.

Remember: RAG finds information. Citations show where supporting information came from.
