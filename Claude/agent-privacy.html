Data Privacy

Data privacy ensures personal and sensitive information is collected, stored, and processed responsibly.

Why It Matters

AI systems may process personal details, financial records, and confidential business information.

Best Practices

  • Minimize data collection.
  • Encrypt sensitive information.
  • Remove unnecessary personal data.
  • Control access permissions.

Remember

Never enter passwords, private keys, personal records, or confidential data into untrusted AI tools.
