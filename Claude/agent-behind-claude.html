Inside the ideas behind Claude.

Alignment, interpretability, and the difficult question of how to build AI systems that behave as intended.

FIELD NOTE 001

Constitutional AI

Constitutional AI is an approach developed by Anthropic that uses an explicit set of principles to help guide model behavior. A training process can include model-generated critiques and revisions rather than relying exclusively on human feedback for every example.

01 — Principles

Define behavioral guidance and safety goals.

02 — Critique

Evaluate a response against relevant principles.

03 — Revision

Use feedback to improve the response or training signal.

The big question

Can AI critique itself reliably?

Self-critique can help, but a model may miss its own errors. Independent evaluation and human oversight remain important.

Alignment is a research problem, not a checkbox.

FIELD NOTE 002

Mechanistic interpretability

Researchers investigate internal model representations and computational circuits to better understand how particular behaviors arise. The goal is to move beyond simply observing outputs.

Research question: which internal features contribute to a model's behavior, and how consistently can they be identified?

FIELD NOTE 003

The limits of confidence

A fluent explanation is not proof that a model's answer is correct. Confidence, factual accuracy, and a faithful explanation of internal computation are different things.

Experiment: compare AI-generated claims with primary sources, counterexamples, and independent tests.
