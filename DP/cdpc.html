Consumer-Driven Contract Pattern

Consumers define expectations that providers must satisfy, ensuring reliable communication between services.

👤

Consumer Defines

Consumers specify the requests, responses, fields, and behaviors they depend on from the provider.

📜

Contract Shared

The expectations are captured as a contract that both consumer and provider teams can validate.

🤝

Provider Verifies

Providers verify that their implementation satisfies every agreed consumer expectation before changes are released.

Objective

Prevent integration failures by validating service expectations early.
