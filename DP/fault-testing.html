Resilience Engineering

Fault Injection Pattern

Deliberately introduce failures such as timeouts, network errors, or service crashes to test resilience.

⏱️

Timeouts

Simulate delayed responses to verify that services handle slow dependencies gracefully.

🌐

Network Errors

Introduce dropped connections or unreachable dependencies to test retry and fallback behavior.

💥

Service Crashes

Stop individual services to validate automatic recovery, failover, and system availability.

Objective

Discover weaknesses before production does.

Inject → Observe → Recover
Controlled failures. Measurable resilience.
