What it reveals

Fault injection testing introduces controlled failures such as service outages, network errors, or resource limits to verify how well a system handles unexpected conditions.

Introduce controlled failures to build stronger, more resilient systems. Designed to test how well a system can recover from unexpected conditions and maintain functionality.
